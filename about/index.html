About Bithues

Our Mission

Bithues helps ordinary people, families, and professionals understand cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, stablecoins, tokenization, and digital ownership — safely and practically.

We cut through the hype. We focus on what actually matters: how to hold crypto securely, how to spot scams, how to evaluate new developments with clear eyes.

Our Approach

Every article we publish:

  • Prioritizes safety, risk awareness, and practical decision support.
  • Explains complex topics in plain, non-hype language.
  • Cites primary sources — not just recycled takes.
  • Includes actionable steps, checklists, or decision frameworks.

Our Editorial Standards

We are transparent about how we work:

  • Content is educational, not personalized financial advice.
  • We do not accept payment for coverage.
  • Affiliate relationships — for example, with hardware wallet makers or crypto tax tools — are disclosed on relevant pages.
  • We update articles when material conditions change.
